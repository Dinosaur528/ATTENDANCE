Combine several phones

On the main phone: tap this and select the other phones' backup files (or their exported CSVs) at once. Scans are added, and a student scanned on more than one phone is counted once, with the earliest time kept. Then tap Export CSV for a single file.

Every scan is saved instantly in two places on this phone: the quick-save and the app database (IndexedDB), plus two rotating backup copies a few seconds later. Export regularly to keep a copy off the device.

Attendance layout (Word or Excel)

No layout imported.

Your Word or Excel file needs a table whose heading row has names like No., ID, Name, Email, Date, Time, Signature. The rows under it are replaced by the scans, and anything below the table (signature lines, notes) moves down. Optional: type {{DATE}} or {{TOTAL}} anywhere in the text. Save as DOCX / XLSX keeps your exact design. Fonts, colours, borders, merged cells and logos in Preview are redrawn from your file. Everything works offline.
